The Lever Method

Four phases. One outcome: more profit.

01
Profit Audit
Days 1-3

Find where the profit is leaking.

In the first three days, we do a full audit of your store, ad account, funnels, and any online presence you have. Not from a template, but from scratch - to identify where customers drop off, where the offer is weak, and where the highest-impact opportunities are hiding.

Conversion funnel analysis

We map every stage of your funnel - ad landing, product pages, cart, checkout - and identify the exact drop-off points costing you profit. We look at heatmaps, scroll depth, session recordings, and exit data.

Identifying the heat points

What's leaking profit right now. What can be fixed straight away. What's going to take more time. We map it all out clearly so you know exactly where you stand and what gets tackled first.

Offer and messaging

When you've been looking at your business for so long, everything starts to look the same. We come in with fresh eyes and fresh ideas - an outsized perspective that spots what you've stopped seeing.

Phase 1 output
A prioritised profit audit report - your store's highest-impact opportunities ranked by expected uplift and effort. This is your roadmap for everything that follows.
02
Hypothesis Build
By End of Week 1

Build the test plan around your profit gaps.

By the end of week one, you have a full hypothesis build in your hands. Every test is documented with what we're changing, why we believe it'll move profit, and how we'll know if it worked. No guesswork, no gut feel - every test earns its place before it goes live.

Hypothesis framework

Each test is documented as: current state → observed problem → proposed change → expected impact on profit. Every hypothesis is tied directly to an insight from Phase 1.

Built around your brand's specific needs

Every brand is different. Maybe it's landing pages, maybe it's the checkout flow, maybe it's something else entirely. We don't apply a one-size-fits-all fix - we identify exactly what your brand needs and act on that.

Brand-safe design and copy

All variants are built to look and feel like your brand - we sharpen the story, we don't replace it. Every word and design choice is grounded in how your brand already talks to its customers.

Phase 2 output
A test backlog of 6-10 prioritised hypotheses, plus completed designs and copy for the first 2-3 tests. Ready to go live.
03
Live Testing
Week 2+

Test fast. Lock winners. Repeat.

We don't dawdle. By week two, tests are live. No lengthy back and forth, no endless approval rounds. We move fast because that's where the profit is - and every week without a test running is a week of leaving money on the table.

High-velocity A/B testing

We run multiple tests simultaneously across your key pages. Each test is statistically significant before we call a winner - no premature conclusions, no false positives. Typically 2-4 tests running at any given time.

Winners go permanent, immediately

When a test wins, it's not shelved in a report. It's deployed as the new default within 24 hours. The uplift is locked in. It keeps earning every day from that point forward - your store gets permanently better.

Weekly reporting

Every week you receive a clear summary: what's live, what's winning, what's next, and what your profit impact looks like so far. No jargon, no dashboards you need training to read. One page, the numbers that matter.

Phase 3 output
Measurable profit uplift by week 8-12. Winning variants live and compounding. A test backlog that keeps improving with every round.
04
Compounding
Week 12+

The gains stack. The store keeps getting better.

CRO isn't a project - it's a permanent competitive edge. By week 12, your store is already a better version of itself. Phase 4 is about turning that momentum into a compounding advantage that your competitors can't catch up to.

Compounding wins

Each winning test raises your conversion baseline. The next test starts from a higher floor. Over 6 months, brands that started at 2.5% conversion are often at 3.5%+ - that's 40% more revenue from the same ad spend, locked in permanently.

Expanding test surface

As we learn what works for your brand and your customers, we expand into new areas: upsell and cross-sell flows, post-purchase sequences, AOV optimisation, and new acquisition landing pages.

A store that outlasts any campaign

Ads stop the moment you stop paying for them. Your store doesn't. Every improvement we make is permanent infrastructure - it earns profit on every single visitor, from every channel, forever.

Phase 4 outcome
A permanently better store that compounds profit over time - not a one-time campaign spike. The brands that stay with us longest see the biggest returns.
