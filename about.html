Peter

Founder, The Last Lever

Peter co-founded Ozzi Gear and spent years scaling it the way most DTC founders do: more ad spend, more channels, more complexity. Revenue grew. Profit didn't keep up.

"We were doing $1.5M a month and I still felt like we were running to stand still. The ads were working. The margin wasn't."

So he started pulling the site apart. Not redesigning it - testing it. Methodically changing what the page said, how it said it, and who it said it to. Persona-targeted landing pages. Sharper offer structures. Conversion flows that matched the way customers actually thought.

Within three months, Ozzi Gear went from $1.5M to $2M a month in revenue. Monthly profit jumped from $300K to $400K - $100K more every month, with zero new ad spend. The same traffic, the same budget, a fundamentally better store.

By the time revenue hit $2.5M a month, Peter had a repeatable system. Not a playbook that worked for one brand, but a methodology built around how DTC customers make decisions and how to convert that into bottom-line profit.

He built The Last Lever to give other ambitious founders access to that same system. Not as a massive agency with 40 clients and a junior account manager on your account - but as a small, focused team that treats your brand the way Peter treated his own.

We take on 4 new clients per month - to make sure every brand gets the attention it deserves. 2 spots remaining in June.

How we work

The principles behind every decision.

Profit over vanity metrics

Every test is filtered through one question: does this improve the bottom line? Not sessions, not clicks, not bounce rate. Profit.

Every brand gets a unique plan

We start with a full audit of your store. No two brands get the same playbook because no two brands have the same conversion problem.

Methodology, not guesswork

High-velocity testing with a 60% win rate. Every test is a hypothesis, not a hunch. Winners stay live and compound. Losers teach us something.

Brand-safe CRO

We sharpen the story your site tells. We don't replace it. Your brand should come out of this feeling more like itself, not like a conversion machine.

Small client base, by design

We cap the number of brands we work with each month. That's not a sales tactic - it's how we make sure your brand gets the attention it deserves.

Results in 8-12 weeks

Not 6-month retainers before you see anything. We're structured to move fast: full audit in week one, first tests live in week two, results you can measure before the quarter ends.
